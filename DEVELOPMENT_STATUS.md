# Development status

The public CliMed release is restricted to Runs 1–3.

Severity/recovery and DENV-1, DENV-2, DENV-3 and DENV-4 serotype-specific probability/dominance functionality are in development and are not part of this public release. They must not be interpreted as validated clinical, diagnostic, surveillance or operational public-health outputs.

Other development modules are not distributed through this repository.
