# Disclaimer

CliMed is a scientific modelling tool. Its outputs are model-derived estimates based on climate, vector, population, land-use and optional user-supplied spatial inputs.

Outputs must not be interpreted as official dengue surveillance counts, confirmed infections, clinical diagnoses, medical advice, or a substitute for decisions by public-health authorities. Interpretation depends on input-data quality, spatial and temporal resolution, parameter assumptions and model uncertainty.

This Runs 1–3 public build does not distribute observed-case fitting or fitted-count products. Severity/recovery and DENV-serotype functionality are in development and are not part of this release.
